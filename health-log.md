# Health Log

Historical measurements recovered from Apple Health are incorporated into this log as ordinary dated health entries. The complete underlying Apple Health export is preserved separately in `apple-health.md`.

### Medication Naming Convention

- Depakote — generic divalproex is actually dispensed; "Depakote" is used in this log as the preferred medication name.

- Seroquel — generic quetiapine is actually dispensed; "Seroquel" is used in this log as the preferred medication name.

- Saphris — brand-name Saphris (asenapine) is actually dispensed and taken.

- Brand-name Saphris is preferred over generic asenapine because its packaging is substantially easier to open and administer.
