# Health Log Index

Health entries are stored in separate files by ISO week so no individual health-log file becomes excessively large.

## Current weekly logs

- `health-log-2026-W36.md` — September 3–6, 2026

## Historical archive

- `health-log-legacy-through-2026-W35.md` — preserved copy of the former monolithic health log through ISO week 35.

## Naming convention

Weekly health-log files use:

`health-log-YYYY-Www.md`

where `YYYY` is the ISO week-numbering year and `ww` is the two-digit ISO week number.
